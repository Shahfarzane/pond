## Important

- Pointer Cursors, Display Color Space, and Image Processing Fallback are gone from Settings.

## New

- Fish have animated faces that follow what they're doing and perk up when you hover or press them.
- Pick from seven new species and 15 colors in each fish's appearance settings.
- Fish replies lay out as full-width turns, and the thread stays on the latest message while it streams.

## Improved

- Nested settings stay visible and dimmed until you turn on the switch above them.
- Busy buttons keep their label and show a spinner beside it.
- Forms check your input when you submit instead of disabling Submit.
- Truncated names in the sidebar, tables, and rows show in full on hover.
- Long lists fade at their edges as they scroll.
- Reorder combined images and note blocks from the keyboard or the context menu.
- Save an Export preset from a popover instead of a second dialog.
- Schedule and Sources show their layout while they load.
- The routine list on a fish uses tighter rows with the switch beside each routine.
- Select fields open with a down chevron.
- Labels and settings descriptions follow one style across Pond.

## Fixed

- Activity metrics on a save line up with its other properties.
- Provider key and endpoint fields no longer spellcheck or autofill.
- Routines show live status while a fish edits them.
