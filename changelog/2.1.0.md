## New

- Watch any RSS or Atom feed from New Watch > RSS Feed, and Pond adds each new entry to your Library without an account.
- Save a Marked ref from its link, or connect Marked to sync the refs in your Marked library.
- Imported saves show where they came from, like Eagle, mymind, or your bookmarks, in an Imported From row and filter.
- Your mouse's back and forward buttons move through Pond the same way the toolbar arrows do.

## Improved

- Pond's occasional asks, like the Cosign Awards vote or Star on GitHub, appear as a toast you can answer when you like.
- Rating, trashing, or deleting a large selection of saves no longer stalls Pond.
- Video enrichments use less CPU and release their memory when they finish.

## Fixed

- Saving a page that's in Trash brings it back to your Library.
- The Safari extension connects to Pond on signed builds.
- Open Safari Settings opens Pond's extension in Safari on signed builds.
- Web saves whose only image is the site icon finish downloading.
- X and Threads replies finish downloading after Pond restarts.
- Disabled switches look and act disabled.
- The master sounds toggle is named All Sounds, so screen readers tell it apart from Interface Sounds.
